Welcome to Story Builder!

Build your own coherent story in 5 stages

🎮 How to Play

Build a story in 5 steps. Pick good choices to make your story make sense!

Step 1: Pick your hero (like Fox, Rabbit, or Bear)

Step 2: Pick a place (like Forest, River, or Castle)

Step 3: Pick a problem (like Lost, Hungry, or Cold)

Step 4: Pick a solution (how to fix the problem)

Step 5: Pick an ending (how the story ends)

🎯 Tip: Good choices = High score!

🌟 Tip: Make choices that make sense together!

📖 Story Example

Here's what a complete story looks like:

🦊 Character: Fox
🌲 Setting: Forest
❓ Problem: Got lost in the dark woods
💡 Action: Followed the stars to navigate
✨ Resolution: Found the way home safely
💡 Why this works: Fox in a Forest makes sense! They belong there. The problem fits the setting, and the solution fits the problem.

🎯 Understanding Coherence

Coherence measures how well your story makes sense!

95% - Perfect! ⭐

🦊 Fox + 🌲 Forest = Makes sense!

60% - Okay 👍

🦊 Fox + 🌊 Ocean = A bit odd

30% - Strange 🤔

🐟 Fish + 🏜️ Desert = Doesn't work!

Higher coherence = Higher score!

Think: Would this really happen in real life?
